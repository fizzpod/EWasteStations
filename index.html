Field guide

What actually counts as e-waste

Points shown are what you'd earn per item logged in CircuitBack — heavier environmental payoff, more points.

Why not the bin

Circuit boards, batteries and screens contain recoverable metals alongside materials that can leach into soil and water if crushed with general waste — proper recycling separates the two.

Where it goes

Collected e-waste is sent to licensed recyclers for dismantling, where reusable metals, plastics and components are recovered instead of ending up incinerated or landfilled.

Battery safety

Tape battery terminals before dropping them off — damaged batteries in transit are a common cause of fires in collection bins.
